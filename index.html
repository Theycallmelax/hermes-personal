Hermes Personal

A private, single-user personal assistant.

What this application does

Hermes Personal is a private, single-user personal assistant. It connects to its owner’s own Google Account using read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar so that it can summarise the owner’s incoming messages and keep track of their schedule and deadlines.

Who it is for

The application is used by one person only — its owner — on their own machine. It is not distributed, it is not offered to the public, and no other user can sign in to it.

What it can see

  • Gmail — read-only. It can read messages to flag anything that needs attention. It cannot send, delete, or modify anything.
  • Google Calendar — read-only. It can read events to build the owner’s schedule. It cannot create, change, or delete events.

No other Google data is accessed, and the data is never shared with anyone else.
